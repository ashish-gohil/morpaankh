Tapi & Co.

A mid-premium ethnic-wear storefront for Surat. This page lists everything in the project so you can jump straight to the right file.

Storefront prototype →
Full clickable storefront — Home, Collection, PDP (interactive size quiz, pincode lookup, fit-feedback bar, cart drawer, size guide modal), About. All product imagery is text-only fabric-swatch placeholders.

5 · What I didn't build (yet)

  • Couple sets — de-scoped per your answer. Easy to add back later.
  • Real product photography — placeholders only. The FabricSwatch component is the rendered placeholder; replace it with <img> once you have shots.
  • Checkout — Shopify handles checkout itself; the prototype's "Checkout" button is a no-op.
  • Real reviews integration — mocked. Wire to Judge.me or Loox later.
  • Live shipping rates — pincode widget is a front-end mock. Marked TODO in the Liquid file.
